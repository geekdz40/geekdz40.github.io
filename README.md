## 📚 About GeekDZ

**GeekDZ** is the official technology club at **Abbas Laghrour University** in Khenchela, Algeria. We are a community of passionate tech enthusiasts dedicated to fostering innovation, knowledge sharing, and professional development in emerging technologies.

As a **Red Hat Academy Partner**, we provide our members with access to cutting-edge training, certifications, and real-world industry experience.

### 🎯 Our Mission
To inspire and empower students to become skilled technologists by providing hands-on workshops, competitions, and mentorship in:
- 💻 **Programming & Software Development**
- 🔒 **Cybersecurity**
- 🤖 **Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning**
- ⛓️ **Blockchain & Web3**
- 🔬 **Scientific Research**
- 🌐 **Web Development**
